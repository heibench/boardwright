# 02 rp2040_min

**Reference:** Raspberry Pi "Hardware design with RP2040", minimal design
example: USB-C with 5.1k CC resistors, 27R USB series resistors, 12 MHz crystal
with 1k series and 15 pF loads, W25Q128 flash, 100 nF per supply pin, 1 uF on
VREG_VIN and VREG_VOUT. Values from memory of that document, not re-read.
The USBLC6 ESD part and AP2112K regulator are my additions.

**Stresses:** 0.4 mm-pitch QFN-56 on two layers, 12 decouplers, USB, crystal.

**What broke**
- 16 connections unrouted: a 0.25 mm track cannot leave a 0.4 mm-pitch pad at
  0.2 mm clearance.
- No room for decouplers on top: QSPI, USB and six supply pins share an edge.
- With GND pours present before routing, Freerouting assumed GND was connected;
  the pours then fragmented: 24 unconnected items, starved thermals.
- NPTH hole clearance on the USB-C receptacle.
- 12 parity errors on no-connect pins.
- The first schematic was an A0 sheet with parts scattered and flags off-sheet.

**Tool changes**
- `CLASSES` net classes; fine-pitch defaults documented.
- Bottom-side placement: `PLACE[ref] = (x, y, rot, "B")`.
- Route everything as traces, pour afterwards (`POUR_FIRST` restores the old order).
- No-connect pads get KiCad's `unconnected-(REF-PIN-PadN)` net name.
- Schematic packer: functional groups, horizontal passives, paper sized to fit.

## Second pass, after the review existed

Adding `REVIEW` limits to this already-passing board failed it on five counts:
the flash decoupler was 8.45 mm from the flash VCC pin, the crystal was 6.3 mm
from XIN with 13.3 mm of XIN trace, and USB D+ was 7.4 mm longer than D-.
None of that shows up in DRC.

- Flash decoupler moved beside pin 8; crystal, its load caps and the XOUT
  series resistor pulled in under the bottom pad row.
- USB D+/D- from the series resistors to pins 47/46 hand-routed as a mirrored
  pair (`TRACKS`).
- First attempt put the series resistors directly over the USB pins. That
  blocked the escape of the neighbouring supply pins to their bottom-side
  decouplers: 9 unconnected. Resistors moved 4 mm out, pair kept straight.
- Freerouting left two tracks 0.1488 mm apart against a 0.15 mm rule. The
  driver now adds 10 um to every clearance in the DSN before routing.

**Left open**
- USB is length-matched from resistors to chip only; the connector side is
  autorouted with a 3 mm skew limit. No impedance target on two layers.
- Crystal load caps not recalculated for a specific crystal.
